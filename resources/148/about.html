To those interested in the Zacatlán-Ahuacatlán-Tepetzintla (Puebla) Nahuatl speech corpus (OpenSLR #148; Glottocode: zaca1241; ISO 639-3: nhi) Since the first publication of this corpus on OpenSLR it has been improved (minor sound editing and new transcriptions). I and my native speaker colleagues have worked on this and and will continue to do so in the coming years. The audio and annotation corpora, and a derivative dataset to faciliate ASR and NLP use of this material, are now available at the Mozilla Data Collective portal where a detailed description of the corpus is available.
  • Zacatlán Tepetzintla Nahuatl Audio https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmlcqxjwl01t8mm07wz7c08bz
  • Zacatlán Tepetzintla Nahuatl Transcriptions https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmlct0jzu01s4nv07023lv3m3
  • Zacatlán Tep5etzintla Nahuatl ASR Dataset https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmls27zfd0043ma07mxvsz8zg
