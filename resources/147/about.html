To those interested in the Orizaba (Veracruz) Nahuatl speech corpus (OpenSLR #147; Glottocode: oriz1235; ISO 639-3: nlv). Since the first publication of this corpus on OpenSLR it has been improved (minor sound editing and new transcriptions). I and my native speaker colleagues have worked on this and and will continue to do so in the coming years. The audio and annotation corpus, and a derivative dataset to faciliate ASR and NLP use of this material are now available at the Mozilla Data Collective portal where a detailed description of the corpus is available.

  • Tequila Zongolica Nahuatl Transcriptions https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmqs1irj8075snr07i69uvzo3
  • Tequila Zongolica Nahuatl ASR Dataset https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmqzs3yig00eens07hehnewvf
